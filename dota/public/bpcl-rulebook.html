THE PLAYER CODE
Be available. Be reachable. Use your verified primary account. Respect teammates, opponents and tournament staff.
Competitive intensity is encouraged. Toxicity, hate, smurfing, unauthorised substitutions and live-game trash talking are not.
SECTION I

Match Operations

01

Match Reporting

  • Each player must report at least 15 minutes before their scheduled match time.
  • A player who has not reported within 15 minutes after the scheduled start time may lose their match slot.
  • The admins may arrange a substitute or replacement. A reasonable delay may occur while a suitable player is identified.
02

Availability

  • All registered players are expected to be available for their scheduled matches.
  • In a genuine emergency, the player must DM the admins as soon as possible.
03

Substitute Rules

  • Each player may take a substitute for a maximum of two games during the tournament.
  • Each team can play with a maximum of 2 substitutes per match. A team missing more than 2 members at their game time will result in a forfeit.
  • Substitute requests must be submitted at least four hours before the scheduled game time.
  • All substitutes will be allocated and approved exclusively by the admins.
  • Captains and players may not directly bring a player of similar MMR, a player from the same tier, or any other replacement without prior admin approval.
  • A player may request a specific substitute, but the request is confirmed only after approval from the admins.
  • In the event of a player no-show, some delay should be expected because a suitable substitute may not be available immediately.
04

Permanent Replacement

Critical rule
  • If a player is unavailable for multiple matches, cannot continue in the tournament, or requires a substitute beyond the two-game limit, the admins may appoint a permanent replacement.
  • Permanent replacements will be selected by the admins with consideration for player tier, MMR and team balance.
SECTION II

Communication & Identity

Players must remain reachable, use verified primary accounts and maintain respectful in-game identities. Resolve account visibility, microphone and Discord issues before match day.

05

Discord & Communication

  • Discord is mandatory for all players.
  • Players must remain reachable before and during their scheduled matches.
  • A working microphone is strongly recommended for team coordination.
  • Players who are unreachable may lose their slot or be replaced by the admins.
06

Account Rules

  • Players must use their primary account.
  • Rank must be unlocked and visible.
  • Smurfing is strictly prohibited.
  • Any player found smurfing will be immediately disqualified from the event.
07

Steam Name Rule

  • Hateful, toxic, racist, offensive or disrespectful Steam names are strictly prohibited.
  • Players must use respectful Steam names during the event.
  • Admins may require a Steam name change before a match. Refusal to comply may result in a warning or disqualification.

08

Game Mode

  • Matches will be played in Captains Mode.
  • The captain will be selected mutually by the team members or decided according to admin instructions.
09

Pause Rules

  • Each team is allowed a maximum of three pauses per match.
  • The total pause time available to each team cannot exceed 10 minutes.
  • The reason for every pause must be communicated to the moderator or admin.
  • Unnecessary pauses, deliberate stalling or pause abuse may result in penalties.
SECTION IV

Conduct & Fair Play

Competition must remain intense, professional and respectful. Live-game misconduct carries direct consequences.

10

Player Behaviour

  • Toxic behaviour is strictly prohibited.
  • When admins receive a complaint, the incident will be reviewed and a warning may be issued.
  • A player may receive up to two warnings. If the behaviour does not improve, the player will be disqualified from the event.
  • Serious live-game misconduct may receive an immediate penalty or disqualification under Rule 11.
11

Live Game Conduct & Trash Talking

Critical rule
  • Trash talking during a live game is strictly prohibited.
  • Any player who trash talks in all chat will receive a time penalty in their next scheduled game. The duration and method of enforcement will be determined by the admins based on the severity of the incident.
  • Trash talking, abuse or toxic behaviour directed at teammates or opponents during a live game may lead to disqualification.
  • Repeated or serious misconduct may be penalized without prior warning.
12

All Chat & Taunting

  • Friendly all chat is allowed.
  • Taunting, abusive or toxic all chat is prohibited.
  • Comments such as "EZ", "?" or similar remarks may be treated as trash talk and penalized under Rule 11.
13

Tipping & Voice Lines

  • In-game tipping and voice lines are allowed.
  • Players are expected to keep the competitive spirit fun and respectful.
14

Respect Policy

  • Players must respect all participants, regardless of rank or skill level.
  • Toxicity, harassment, personal attacks or repeated disrespect toward players, captains, admins or moderators will not be tolerated.
15

Zero Hate Policy

Critical rule
  • Anyone trying to create a hateful environment during the matches or on the Discord server will be banned permanently.
  • Any hate directed towards the admins will result in a permanent ban, and forfeit of the event if the person is currently playing in the event.
  • You are allowed to give your opinions, but it has to be done respectfully.
SECTION V

Administration

16

Emergency Cases

  • In genuine emergencies, players may DM the admins and request support.
  • The admins will review the circumstances and make the final decision.
17

Admin Decision

  • The admins' decision will be final in all cases.
  • This includes match disputes, replacements, substitutions, toxicity and hate reports, smurfing checks, time penalties, bans, disqualifications and all tournament rulings.
  • Players must cooperate with schedules, moderators and tournament instructions.
